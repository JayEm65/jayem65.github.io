For You

Dear Eleftheria, Every time I thought I had finally found the courage to send this, I realized there was still something missing, or I hadn't expressed it the way I truly meant it. Maybe that is because this isn't just a letter. It is my attempt to share honestly what is still in my heart, while also knowing that, in the end, I cannot and do not want to control what you do with any of it. I am not writing this to pressure you or convince you into a decision. I am writing because I couldn't live with myself if I never tried to honestly tell you what is in my heart. If there is still a part of you that wonders whether there was more we could have done, I hope you will read this with an open heart. And if there isn't, I hope you will simply receive it as what it is: one person trying to be completely honest with someone he still loves. I finally developed the films from Rhodes after carrying them around for weeks. Ironically, many of the photos did not turn out the way I hoped. Some were damaged by the airport scanner, some were missing entirely, and others captured only fragments of what I wanted to remember. Still, I wanted to share them with you, because those memories belong to you too.
~✦~
Looking through them reminded me that even if photographs fade, the moments themselves did not disappear. I still remember them with warmth. More than anything, I remember how you made me feel. You brought a kind of peace into my life that I had not experienced before. I never felt like I had to perform around you. I never felt like I had to earn your affection. I loved our conversations, our long walks, eating together, sitting quietly without needing to fill every silence, laughing about small things, exploring our hometowns together, searching the sky for a while–watching the stars and the moon, the beach, and discovering your little world through your eyes. Waking up next to you. I remember the moments when we both let our guard down. The way you caught yourself looking at me. The way you made me let my own guard down and simply smile back at you. Those ordinary moments became extraordinary because they were ours. You made me feel seen. Not because you always had the perfect words, but because you noticed the little things. The memories you carried. Your curiosity. The thoughtful gestures. The details you remembered that I thought only I had noticed. You loved quietly, and I learned to recognize that. Those memories are still with me.
~✦~
That is why losing us has been one of the hardest things I have experienced. Over the last months, I have spent a lot of time reflecting—not only on what happened between us, but on myself. I have tried very hard not to reduce our story into one where one person was right and the other person was wrong. I do not believe that is what happened. I know that during our last week together, we both became overwhelmed. I became afraid of losing you, and instead of slowing down, I became more intense. My fear became louder than my patience. Louder than my ability to simply stay present with you. For that, I am truly sorry. The moment when you had your panic attack has stayed with me. I understand that my actions in that moment felt frightening and overwhelming for you. That is painful for me to acknowledge, because frightening you was never something I wanted. In that moment, I was acting from panic and desperation to reconnect, reaching for you because I wanted to comfort you. But I understand now that my intention does not erase the impact. I am sorry. If that moment changed the way you saw me, I understand why. I know that trust can be affected by moments like that, and I am deeply sorry for the fear I caused you. I hope you can also believe me when I say that I never wanted to hurt you. Not physically. Not emotionally. Never.
~✦~
There is something else I have wanted to express. Not to defend myself. To explain myself. Those are different things. I am not asking you to see everything exactly as I saw it. I only hope you can understand what was happening inside me too. Because my feelings do not erase yours. And yours do not erase mine. There were moments toward the end where I felt lost and hurt because I was trying to understand things that felt bigger than the moment between us. Sometimes I became too focused on finding explanations instead of simply staying with what was happening between us right then. I know your experiences shaped you, and I never wanted to hold them against you. I wanted to understand you. I wanted to be someone who felt safe. I never wanted to rescue you. I wanted to stand beside you. Support you. Care for you. I believed we could heal alongside each other by meeting each other honestly, staying curious about each other, and continuing to choose each other even when things became difficult. That was the relationship I believed we were trying to build. Maybe that belief was naive. Maybe it still is. But it was real for me.
~✦~
I also don't want this letter to erase the moments where we did try. I remember asking each other what was on our minds. I remember you caring about understanding me, asking questions, and trying to see things from my perspective. I remember us apologizing when we realized something had hurt the other person. I remember us taking time to reflect, giving each other space, and trying to understand each other's feelings better. Those moments mattered to me. I don't want to remember only the moments where we failed each other in the end. I do not think our final weeks showed who we truly were at our best. I think they showed two people who cared deeply about each other but became overwhelmed by everything happening around them. Stress. Fear. Distance. Life. I do not believe our ending erased everything that came before it. And that is why a part of me still wonders whether we gave up before we had found our way through.
~✦~
I do not know what story you carry about us today. Maybe you're trying to find peace. Maybe you are still hurt. Maybe you are conflicted. Maybe you have moved on. Maybe you still carry pieces of us that I cannot see. I honestly do not know. And I do not want to pretend that I do. What I do know is that meeting you changed something in me. Before we met, I wrote that you must be the thread between dream and memory. I still think about those words. Not because I want to hold onto a fantasy, but because opening up to you showed me that I was capable of trusting, hoping, and imagining a future with you. When we talked about Rhodes and about building a life together, it never felt like a sacrifice to me. It felt like choosing something that already mattered to me. I knew we were both in a phase of our lives where things were changing. You were building your life in Rhodes, finishing your school, working, and being close to your family. I was finding my own stability again and creating a foundation for myself. But I never experienced that chapter of our lives as something that was pulling us apart. To me, it felt like two people growing through a transition while still feeling connected and imagining what could come next. When we talked about our future, whether that meant Rhodes or somewhere else we would discover together, it felt real to me. Not because every detail was already decided, but because I felt that we were both opening the door to that possibility. And if I am completely honest, underneath it all, I can still feel it. Still loving you. That has not simply disappeared for me because we ended. I still believe that what we had was real and meaningful. If there is ever a possibility to rebuild something with more understanding, more patience, more safety, and more openness from both of us, I would like that very much. Not because I believe love fixes everything. Not because I believe we could simply return to how things were. But because I still believe what we had was worth caring for. At the same time, I know that love cannot be demanded. I cannot ask you to carry my hope or make a decision because of my feelings. I can only be honest about where I am. I hope that somewhere beneath everything that happened, there is still a part of you that remembers us with warmth. But if there is not, I will do my best to respect that too. If there is still something left to say between us, or something in your heart you would like to share, I would welcome that. Slowly. Carefully. Honestly. Not by pretending the past did not happen, but by acknowledging it and learning from it. And if your heart knows there is no path back, no spark left, I hope you can tell me that honestly as well. Not because I want to argue with your decision. But because clarity allows people to grieve what is real.
~✦~
If I could go back to the beginning... To our first conversations. To Rhodes. To meeting you for the first time. To feeling your hug for the first time. To asking if I could hold your pinky. To all those ordinary moments that became extraordinary. I would still choose to know you. I would still choose to open my heart to you. I would simply try to carry that love with less fear. You became a place of peace for me. Your embrace gave me the feeling of homecoming. I will always be grateful for that feeling and for the moments we created together. Until then, I will carry what we shared with gratitude, even alongside the sadness. Take care of yourself. Toujours, Marc
